1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Little Forge Games handles information in connection with Emberwake Idle for Android and this support website. Little Forge Games is the developer name used for the game. For privacy questions and requests, contact littleforgegamesinfo@gmail.com.

The game uses local gameplay saves, Google Play Billing, a subscription-verification service operated for Little Forge Games on Google Cloud, and Google AdMob with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising privacy choices. You do not need a separate Little Forge Games account to play.

2. Gameplay data and Android backup

The game stores progress and settings in the app's storage on your device, including levels and experience, inventory and equipment, combat and production progress, Tasks, Quests, Achievements, offline-progression timestamps, purchase entitlements, subscription status and expiry information, and boost timers. Recent reward identifiers are stored locally to prevent duplicate rewards.

Little Forge Games does not upload your gameplay save to its subscription verifier and does not provide its own cross-device gameplay cloud-save service. Purchase verification is separate from saving or restoring gameplay progress.

Android system backup is separate. Depending on your device and settings, Android may back up or transfer eligible app data using your backup provider. The app excludes its subscription purchase-token cache from Android cloud backup and device transfer; eligible gameplay and entitlement records may still be included. Little Forge Games does not control your system backup service.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling can erase local progress and settings. Backup copies are controlled through your device or backup provider and may remain separately. Restoring a purchase does not restore your gameplay progress.

3. Google Play purchases and subscriptions

Google Play processes optional purchases. Emberwake Premium is a monthly, automatically renewing subscription for the listed gameplay benefits while access is valid. Permanent Ad-free is a separate one-time purchase that skips advertising for covered current and future ad-supported features and grants their usual benefit; normal boost timers still apply. Ad-free does not include the monthly membership.

The app receives purchase information from Google Play, including product IDs, purchase tokens, payment or purchase status, acknowledgement status, and signed purchase data with signatures. It uses this information to verify purchases, grant benefits, acknowledge completed purchases, and restore eligible ownership. Permanent product IDs and subscription access decisions are kept locally so previously verified benefits can be used subject to their access limits.

Google handles your payment method and transaction records. Little Forge Games does not receive your full payment-card details through the game or subscription verifier. The game does not ask you to send your Google password or full payment-card details.

See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Hosted subscription verification

When checking a subscription, the app sends its purchase token, subscription product ID, and base-plan ID over HTTPS to Little Forge Games' verifier hosted on Google Cloud Run. A purchase token identifies a Google Play purchase and is used to look up whether access is valid. The request does not include your gameplay save, full payment-card details, or a separate Little Forge Games account profile.

The verifier sends the token to the Google Play Developer API to obtain the subscription record, including product and plan information, purchase and acknowledgement state, and start and expiry times. It acknowledges verified paid purchases when required. It returns a limited product/plan, access-status, validity-period, and verification-time result to the game. The game uses this result to activate, renew, restore, or end subscription access. Google Play subscription records may contain additional transaction information, which the verifier processes to produce this limited response.

The app stores a limited cache of subscription tokens privately on the device to retry verification and restore access after connection problems. This cache remains until app data is cleared or removed, or entries are replaced by later purchases. Cached subscription access ends at its saved expiry unless a later successful verification extends it. Offline operation cannot immediately learn about a refund or other status change made at Google Play.

The current verifier processes purchase requests in memory and does not maintain a persistent purchase-token database. Its application code does not deliberately write purchase tokens or request bodies to logs. This does not mean the service handles no personal information: the token and associated subscription record are processed during verification, and hosting metadata is handled as described below.

Hosting metadata and logs

Google Cloud receives the network request and automatically creates service request logs. These can include your IP address, request time, requested endpoint, response status, request size, latency, and user-agent information. Little Forge Games may access operational logs to diagnose failures, secure the verifier, and prevent abuse. The verifier's HTTPS request body carries the token; the app does not put it in the verifier URL.

The verifier is deployed in Google Cloud's Finland region (europe-north1). This is its hosting location, not a promise that all related Google processing stays in Finland. Google Play, Google Cloud, and their subprocessors may process information in other locations under the applicable service terms and data-protection arrangements.

See Google Cloud's Privacy Notice and Cloud Data Processing Addendum.

5. Cancellation, refunds and local deletion

Premium benefits become available after payment verification. Your subscription renews monthly unless canceled through Google Play. Canceling stops future renewals; access normally continues until the end of the paid period. Cancellation does not automatically refund the current period. Refund eligibility is governed by Google Play's refund policies and applicable consumer law.

Use Manage / cancel subscription in Google Play on the game's Premium screen or Google Play's subscription settings. To ask about a purchase that was not delivered or does not work as described, contact support. See Google Play's refund guidance.

Deleting data does not cancel billing. Clearing local app storage, resetting game progress, uninstalling, or asking us to delete support correspondence does not by itself cancel a Google Play subscription or erase Google's transaction history. Manage the subscription separately through Google Play.

6. Rewarded advertising and consent

The game offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. Where available, completing an ad grants the selected game benefit. Google's User Messaging Platform requests advertising privacy choices where required; consent and privacy-choice records may be stored on the device and processed by Google's services.

Google's advertising services may collect and share IP addresses, device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. An IP address may be used to estimate a general location. These services use data for ad delivery, personalization where permitted, measurement and analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Processing depends on your choices, device settings, and how an ad is served.

Google and participating advertising partners may store or access information on your device. The consent message provides information about partners and purposes. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent processing needed for other permitted ad functions.

You can review available options using Ad privacy choices in the game's Premium and Boosts screen, Android advertising settings, and Google My Ad Center. Availability and effects vary by device and region. Buying Ad-free skips ad viewing for covered features; it does not stop Google Play purchase verification or delete data already processed by advertising services.

See How Google uses information for advertising and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services.

7. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to include, such as an app version, device details, screenshots, or a purchase order reference. We use these to answer questions, investigate problems, handle purchase issues, and respond to privacy requests. Support email is handled through Gmail, which also processes it under Google's service practices.

Please send only information relevant to your request. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details, purchase tokens, or unrelated personal information.

GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to host and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Clicking an email link opens your email service. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

9. Purposes, recipients and legal bases

We process the information described here to provide the game and purchased benefits, verify subscriptions and prevent duplicate rewards or abuse, operate and secure the verifier, support advertising privacy choices, respond to requests, and comply with legal obligations. We do not operate a business selling your gameplay saves, purchase tokens, or support correspondence.

Recipients include Google Play and Google Payments for billing; Google Cloud for hosting and verification; Google AdMob and the advertising partners identified in the consent experience; Gmail for support; GitHub for this website; and any system-backup provider you use. Information is handled by these services for the purposes described. We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to address a legal claim or protect users and the service.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing needed to deliver and verify a purchase is based on providing the service you request. We rely on consent for advertising or device-access processing that requires it, legitimate interests for activities such as proportionate security, fraud prevention and support, and legal obligations where applicable. Consent can be withdrawn using available privacy controls or by contacting us, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Google and GitHub explain their own roles and legal bases in their policies.

Providers may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Their applicable terms and notices describe international processing and safeguards. Access to our operational information is limited to people and providers who need it for the stated purposes.

10. Retention

  • Local gameplay and entitlement data: kept on the device until changed, reset, cleared, or removed; backup copies are managed separately. Subscription tokens remain in a limited local retry cache until replaced or app data is removed.
  • Verifier requests: purchase tokens and retrieved records are processed in memory for the request rather than stored in a separate persistent purchase database.
  • Cloud request and operational logs: retained under the Google Cloud project's logging-retention settings and applicable platform policies. We use operational records for troubleshooting, security, fraud prevention, and legal obligations, and do not deliberately keep additional identifiable copies beyond those purposes. Exact platform retention depends on the record type and service configuration.
  • Support correspondence: retained as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, handle related follow-up or disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Google Play transactions, advertising records, GitHub hosting records and system backups: retained by those services according to their policies, your settings and applicable legal requirements.

11. Your choices, deletion and privacy rights

You can clear local app storage in Android settings, but this can permanently erase progress, settings, and locally cached purchases or tokens. Restoring eligible purchases through Google Play is separate from restoring a gameplay save. Manage Android backups separately through your device or backup-provider settings.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information handled by Little Forge Games, email littleforgegamesinfo@gmail.com. We may need reasonable information to verify and locate your request. We cannot remotely access or erase a save that exists only on your device. Some records may need to be retained to comply with law or resolve a dispute.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. If you are in Lithuania, you can contact the State Data Protection Inspectorate. These rights apply subject to the relevant legal conditions.

For independently held Google, GitHub or backup-provider information, use their privacy controls or contact them directly. We cannot erase their independent account or transaction records. Deletion of our information does not itself cancel Google Play billing; use the subscription controls described above.

Google Play age restrictions, parental controls and purchase approval settings may apply separately. Advertising privacy choices and any age-related handling by Google are subject to the applicable requirements and settings.

If we learn that personal information has been handled without consent required by applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address it. This privacy policy does not replace the game's app-store content rating or its audience settings.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled, including restricting service access and avoiding deliberate token logging, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and Google account.
