1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Little Forge Games handles information in connection with Emberwake Idle for Android and this support website. Little Forge Games is the developer name used for the game. For privacy questions and requests, contact littleforgegamesinfo@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current game implementation, which uses local gameplay saves, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising privacy choices.

2. Gameplay data &amp; Android backup

The game stores progress and settings in the app's storage on your device. This includes levels and experience, inventory, equipment, combat and production progress, Tasks, Quests, Achievements, offline-progression timestamps, purchase-unlock state, and boost timers. The game also keeps recent reward identifiers locally to avoid granting the same rewarded boost twice.

The current version does not require you to create a Little Forge Games account and does not upload your gameplay save to a server operated by Little Forge Games. It does not provide its own cross-device cloud-save service.

Android backup is separate from game cloud saves. The app permits Android system backup and device transfer. Depending on your Android version, device manufacturer, and backup settings, app data may be copied to your backup provider or transferred to another device. Little Forge Games does not control those system backups.

Local data is kept until it is changed or removed, such as by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Backup copies may remain according to your provider's settings. Restoring purchases does not restore a gameplay save.

3. Google Play purchases

Google Play processes optional purchases. The app receives purchase-related information, such as product identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens, acknowledgement status, and signed purchase data, to verify, grant, and restore permanent features. Owned product identifiers are stored in the local save so purchased features can remain available offline.

Little Forge Games does not receive your full payment-card details through the game. Google handles payment information and its purchase records under its own policies. Removing local app data does not cancel a purchase or delete Google's transaction history.

See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Rewarded advertising &amp; consent

The game offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. Where available, a completed rewarded ad activates the boost you selected in the game. Google's User Messaging Platform requests advertising privacy choices where required.

Google's advertising services may collect and share IP addresses, device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. An IP address may be used to estimate a general location. These services use data for ad delivery and personalization where permitted, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Their processing depends on your choices, device settings, and the services used to serve an ad.

Google and advertising partners may store or access information on your device. The consent message provides information about participating partners and processing purposes. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all data processing needed for other permitted ad functions.

You can review available choices using Ad privacy choices in the game's Premium screen. You can also manage advertising settings in Android and through Google My Ad Center. Availability and effects vary by region and device. See How Google uses information for advertising and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services.

5. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use this information to answer questions, investigate problems, handle purchase issues, and respond to privacy requests. Support email is handled through Gmail and is also subject to Google's service practices.

We retain correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request, handle related follow-up or disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, or unrelated personal information.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, contact forms, or cookies to these pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, as part of hosting and securing the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Clicking an email link opens your email service. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

7. Purposes, sharing &amp; international processing

Information is processed to provide the game and purchased features, support advertising choices, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and comply with legal requirements. We do not operate a business selling your gameplay saves or support correspondence.

Information is handled by the services described above where needed for these purposes. We may also disclose support information when required by law or necessary to address a legal claim or protect users and the service.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for processing that requires it, providing the service you request, legitimate interests such as answering support requests and preventing abuse, or applicable legal obligations, according to the activity. Google and GitHub describe their own legal bases and roles in their policies.

Service providers may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Their privacy notices explain processing locations and applicable international-transfer safeguards. Provider-controlled data is retained under their respective policies.

8. Your choices, deletion &amp; privacy rights

You can clear the game's local storage in Android's app settings. This can permanently erase progress and settings. Android backup data must be managed separately through your device or backup-provider settings; deleting a local save may not remove a backup.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by Little Forge Games, email littleforgegamesinfo@gmail.com. We may need reasonable information to verify and respond to your request. We cannot remotely access or erase a save that exists only on your device.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Some records may need to be retained for legal reasons.

For information held independently by Google, GitHub, or your backup provider, use their privacy controls or contact them directly. We cannot erase their independent account or transaction records.

9. Children &amp; family privacy

Parents or guardians with questions about a child's use of the game or information supplied to support can contact us at the email address above. Please do not send a child's personal information unnecessarily. If we learn that information has been handled without consent required by applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

10. Security &amp; policy changes

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device and Google account protected.

We may update this policy when game features, services, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
